A 5-minute meditation

5 min

Let this be a quiet return to yourself—not another task to perfect.

  1. 01Settle in. Place one hand on your heart and one on your belly.
  2. 02Inhale for 4 counts and exhale for 6. Repeat five times.
  3. 03Soften your jaw, shoulders, belly, hips and hands.
  4. 04Choose one affirmation below and repeat it slowly.
  5. 05Close by thanking your body for one thing it helped you do today.

Words to meet yourself with

Say them aloud, write one down, or choose the words you most need when you look in the mirror.

“I love the skin I’m in.”
“I love and honor myself, inside and out.”
“When I look in the mirror, I choose to see how beautiful I am.”
“I speak to myself and my body with kindness, patience and respect.”
“My worth is not measured by a number, a size or a reflection.”
“I can shape my body while loving it fully today.”
